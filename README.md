# Prisco Lab @ IGB-CNR

Welcome to the GitHub repository of the **Prisco Lab (Immunobiology Lab)**, led by Antonella Prisco at the *Institute of Genetics and Biophysics “Adriano Buzzati-Traverso”* of the *National Research Council of Italy (IGB-CNR)*.

The IGB is a multidisciplinary CNR institute in Naples, dedicated to understanding the molecular and biophysical foundations of cell identity, development, immunology, and disease.

Our lab works at the interface between experimental immunology and computational modeling, with a special interest in immunological memory, vaccine-induced immune responses, and the quantitative modeling of inter-individual variability in immune outcomes.
